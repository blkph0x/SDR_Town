## Next gates, in order

1. **Satellite field qualification (ISS-0016/0020):** replay the same tester IQ
   through SDR Town and JAERO, compare validated units, AES, selected channel,
   voice words and PCM. Use the new per-channel monitor and local JSONL to
   distinguish RF/modem failure from no assignment/audio. Do not accept tone,
   synthetic speech or a PCM sample count as proof of clear conversation.
2. **10 MS/s headroom (ISS-0032):** profile shared downconversion/NCO cost;
   qualify a coarse channelizer/PFB only with exact channel isolation and
   reference-IQ/PCM comparisons. Existing 2.048 MS/s measurements do not prove
   10 MS/s reliability. Larger queues are not a repair for sustained overload.
3. **Independent devices (ISS-0031/T-0062):** implement generation-safe
   per-device ownership and mode bindings from MULTI_SDR_SESSIONS.md. Test
   cancellation, unplug, reopen and stale callbacks before concurrent modes.
   Do not remove the ownership rejection without replacing the ownership model.
4. **Hardware acceptance (ISS-0018/0023/0024/0027):** RSPdx antenna/gain/bias-T
   and direct-sampling device matrix need affected-PC checks. Cached readback
   and a packaged plugin are not physical voltage/reception proof.
5. **Remaining receive-chain work (ISS-0012/0013):** satellite carrier-loss
   release, qualified APT channel profile, SSTV line-sync false partials,
   extended/headerless RF auto, digital SSTV interoperability, tuner clamp
   acknowledgement and long-duration HF load tests remain substantive gaps.
6. **Service/metadata acceptance (ISS-0017):** outside-LAN consented diagnostics
   receipt and collector retention/rate hardening remain separate gates.
   Aircraft photos/registration database and aircraft-ID-only ADS-C reports
   need source/licence/provenance work; no fabricated UI identity substitutes.

Older P25 architectural/specification and continuity issues remain historical
open gates, not justification to alter the currently accepted P25 path here.
